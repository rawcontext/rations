# Rations

*If you have to ask when it resets, you’re already in line.*

A menu bar app for the permanent underclass. Keep tabs on your Codex, Claude,
Antigravity, Grok, and Cursor allowances while the frontier-model overlords decide
when you may think again. See what’s left, when it resets, and how long until your
next attempt to escape the permanent underclass.

## Installation

1. [Download Rations for macOS](https://github.com/rawcontext/rations/releases/latest/download/Rations.dmg).
2. Open the disk image and drag **Rations** into **Applications**.
3. Open Rations, click its menu bar icon, and connect your accounts in **Settings**.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Sign in to the
provider apps or command-line tools you want Rations to track.
